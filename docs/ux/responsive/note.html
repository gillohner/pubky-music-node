Pubky Music on a phone, a tablet and a desktop

One set of parts, arranged three ways. Now playing fits one phone screen; queue, lyrics, details, sources and tags open as sheets. Nothing gets built until you've looked at this.

Rules the whole app follows

  • Screens don't scroll; lists do. Now playing, the quality sheet and the zone picker fit the screen. Library, album, artist, playlist and search scroll, and their headers fold into a compact bar.
  • Secondary things are sheets. Queue, Lyrics, Details (signal path, file, credits), Sources (also on) and Tags open over Now playing: a bottom sheet on a phone, a side panel on a tablet, a docked panel on a desktop.
  • Thumbs first. 44 px targets; the transport and the secondary row in the bottom half; swipe a row for Play next, long-press for its menu.
  • Same parts at every width. A track row, an album tile, the player and a sheet are the same components; only the arrangement and the density change.
  • Safe areas, light and dark, reduced motion, and Android font scale 1.3 without clipping.

Colour from where it plays

While a song plays, the player takes an accent from where it streams: the quality label, the scrubber, the source chip and the play button. Now playing also gets a soft gradient from the cover. Local files and your homeserver keep our own accent. A tint only: no logos beyond the small source mark, no copied layouts or fonts. Lists and the rest of the app keep their own theme, so nothing else changes colour from song to song.

Each accent is checked in both themes and nudged where it fails: text on the background at least 4.5:1, and the play button's ink on the accent at least 4.5:1. On dark, bright accents (Spotify's green, TIDAL's cyan) take dark ink; white on them would fail. Switch the theme to see both. Settings › Appearance: Colour from source (on), Colour from album art (on), or neither. With reduced motion the colour changes without a fade.

Layout, per person

Settings › Home and layout: Layout Automatic (by width, as above), Compact (phone arrangement at any width), or Comfortable (tablet arrangement on a desktop); Dense lists on tablets and desktops; Home rows to show and their order. With per-person libraries (STEWARD-MULTI-USER.md) these are saved on each person's homeserver, so they follow you to every device.

Multi-user screens, inside this shell

  • Library: "My library" first; each library shared with you as its own section (a chip on phones, a sidebar group on desktops), never merged into yours. A shared song's menu says whose library it is from.
  • Settings › My accounts: your TIDAL, Spotify, Qobuz, Last.fm, Plex… from your sources vault; nobody else on the node can use them.
  • Settings › Service apps (admin): the node's app keys (Spotify app, Qobuz app, YouTube key, Last.fm app…) in one screen, each with where to get it and a Test button. People never see it.
  • Settings › My library and sharing: where your files are (and the remote homeserver's quota warning), and who may listen or download.

Build order once you agree

  1. Navigation shell: tab bar, rail, sidebar; the mini player; expand and collapse.
  2. Now playing on one screen; the five sheets and panels.
  3. Library, album, artist, playlist, search: folding headers, grids on tablets, columns on desktops.
  4. Settings as a grouped list on phones, two panes on bigger screens (with the multi-user screens as the ADR lands).

Each step: screenshots at 390, 820 and 1440 px in light and dark, before and after; the overlap, accessibility and click-every-button checks at all three widths.

Three things to decide
  1. The fourth tab: Zones (where it plays, as drawn), or Queue (then Zones moves into the player's zone pill only)?
  2. Swipe gestures on rows: swipe right for Play next and left for Add to playlist, as drawn, or long-press only?
  3. Lyrics: in place of the art (Apple Music) or in the sheet with the others (as drawn)?
